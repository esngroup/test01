    Your data and privacy

    Before continuing, choose whether this demo may send the following information to its configured Telegram chat. If you agree, the app sends device/network details first; later steps follow as you register and request your location.

    • Device and browser details, hardware, battery, storage, and available sensors.
    • Public IP address and approximate IP-based location.
    • Your registration name and the profile photo you choose to capture or upload.
    • Precise GPS coordinates and an approximate address after you request location.
    After you agree, tapping the profile preview or the Camera option requests camera permission; if granted, up to two frames are captured and sent before the profile step. Your selected profile details/photo are sent when you finish registration. GPS is requested only after you tap “Allow Location”; those coordinates and the approximate address are then sent. IP details use external lookup services. Map lookups also contact OpenStreetMap if you decline Telegram sharing. You can change this choice from the shield button on the map; changing it cannot recall data already sent.
